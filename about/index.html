About

This independent micro-publication explains how a prospective user can evaluate a dealer or source route before moving into an account-creation task.

Its purpose is narrow: help readers retrace a source, evaluate relationship claims, limit sensitive-data exposure, notice contact changes, and stop when evidence conflicts.

It does not claim to be BetPro, BPExch, a dealer, an administrator, an operator, or an authorized representative.
