Editorial Policy

We separate source-verification guidance from account creation, login recovery, app installation, payment procedures, and operator-business topics.

Claims

We avoid unsupported claims about authorization, licensing, guaranteed approval, guaranteed winnings, fixed payment terms, fixed verification requirements, or physical offices.

Updates

When a route or operational detail may change, readers are directed to recheck the maintained source rather than rely on a copied message or screenshot.

Corrections

Material corrections should be made transparently and the modified date should be updated when the article meaningfully changes.
